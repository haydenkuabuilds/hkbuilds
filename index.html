Background

AI tools and automation, built to be dependable.

I'm a programmer based in Malaysia. I build AI tools, automation scripts and bots, both for clients and for my own projects.

I can build AI work in two ways. Local projects run on your own hardware, which keeps data private and avoids usage fees. API-based projects connect to hosted services, which suits cases where you want strong results without maintaining the infrastructure. I'll recommend whichever fits your project.

Commissions go through Fiverr: scripts, scrapers, Discord bots and AI integrations. I don't take robotics or hardware projects.
